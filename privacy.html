Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. Your privacy and the confidentiality of your career and organizational data are paramount to us.

1. Information We Collect

Hiring Dreams collects information necessary to deliver professional IT recruitment, staffing, and consulting services. This includes personal contact details (name, email, phone number), employment history, resumes, LinkedIn profiles, and company hiring requirements submitted through our contact forms or email inquiries.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Evaluate candidate qualifications for active and future technical requisitions.
  • Connect prospective job candidates with verified employer client partners.
  • Respond to client staffing inquiries and deliver customized proposals.
  • Comply with applicable legal, labor, and tax regulations in the United States.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or lease candidate or client data to third-party advertisers. Candidate resumes and details are only shared with potential employers with prior candidate knowledge and authorization.

4. Contact Us Regarding Your Data

If you have questions regarding your data, wish to update your resume, or want your information removed from our recruitment database, please email info@hiringdreams.com.
