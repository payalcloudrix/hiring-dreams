Frequently Asked Questions by Clients

Everything you need to know about partnering with Hiring Dreams.

How quickly can Hiring Dreams present qualified candidates?
On average, we present 2 to 3 pre-screened, interview-ready candidate profiles within 48 to 72 hours of receiving your job description and technical requirements.
What is your candidate placement guarantee?
We offer a comprehensive replacement guarantee. If a candidate does not meet your technical expectations or cultural standards during the initial evaluation period, we will immediately replace the resource at zero extra charge.
Do you support onsite, hybrid, and remote roles across the US?
Yes. We place engineers across all 50 US states for remote, hybrid, or onsite roles, handling payroll, tax compliance, and background screening seamlessly.

We provide Contract Staff Augmentation, Contract-to-Hire, Direct Permanent Placement, and Dedicated Managed Engineering Squads tailored to your budget and project timeline.
